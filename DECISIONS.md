## D97: Category icons are Phosphor names stored as "ph:<name>"
Category.iconName already existed (imported Paddhu names). A picked icon is saved as `ph:<phosphor-name>` so imported names are never overwritten by a clash and are simply ignored when drawing. Search matches the start of any word in the label or keywords; every typed word must match. 51 icons, chosen for everyday Indian spending; the list lives in CategoryIconCatalog and tools/gen_icons.py (keep both in step). "Plain tag" clears it.


## D98: Rules on imports are suggestions; Split in Quick Add
Rules fill only an empty category on Ping/UPI/CSV review rows, only with a live category of the right kind (expense vs income), and never change a row's tick: you still look at every row before saving (D94). Quick Add gets a Split button once the entry is ready (not for transfers); it opens the same split sheet as Entry detail and saves one entry per line (D85).

## D99: Background receiver; notification scanning dropped
Master section 7 lists both. Decided with the user on 13 Nov 2026: Ping already sends the bank SMS Chitta should add, and Chitta reads payment screenshots from all UPI apps, so Chitta does **not** read notifications. It can be asked for later; nothing of it is left in the code (the listener, the app ticklist and its parser were built, tested and then removed before release).
- **Receiver** `com.mansaketh.chitta.action.ADD` is exported (other apps must reach it) but ignores everything unless the person made a secret (32 hex characters, shown once). Wrong secret: dropped silently. Right secret but amount, account or category not clear (names must match yours; no guessing; type only expense or income): refused with a notice that says why. A good add saves with a 10-second Undo notice. Transfers are not accepted.
- The secret is kept in the phone's small settings file, outside the book and its backups.
- The notification-scan templates table in Paddhu is not rebuilt; rules (D86) cover categories.
